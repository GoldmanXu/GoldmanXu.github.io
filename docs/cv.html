Draft-and-Render: Mixed-Resolution Distillation for Video Super-Resolution · Sole first author · Ongoing

  • A four-step diffusion distillation framework for 480p-to-720p video super-resolution: three 480p restoration steps followed by one 720p reconstruction step (LLLH).
  • All four steps share one student model and use the original low-quality video as a condition, unifying distillation and super-resolution in one training framework.
  • Estimated backbone compute is about 45% of a four-step, all-720p baseline (approximately 55% less), or about 1.8 times that of a single 720p step.
